# CS01 — Signed five-minute candle runs and conditional continuation

**2026-10-14 · Frozen specification before historical execution · HISTORICAL_EXPLORATORY.**

## Research question

Across all available Binance spot close-to-close five-minute returns through **2026-10-14 00:00 UTC** (only complete UTC days), how often do consecutive upward and downward streaks of each **actual** length occur? Does the probability of next-bar continuation or reversal depend on the **current live streak length** beyond both a 50/50 independent-sign benchmark, the empirically measured nonzero-bar sign balance, and a first-order sign-only Markov model?

The analysis does **NOT** cut data into arbitrary fixed 10-bar windows; one stream per asset is scanned sequentially from beginning to end. A streak of 18 bars is recorded as length 18. Frequency labels are signed (`+1`, `+2`, ..., `-1`, `-2`, ...). The descriptive distribution counts *completed* episodes; the forecasting risk set counts every occasion when an *ongoing* streak has reached length `k` and the next contiguous bar can be observed. These are different denominators. There is no chosen maximum streak length in either table or model fitting.

## Data and definitions

- Source: existing VD14 price loader `load_returns`, Binance spot 5-minute close data under `DERIBIT_DATA_ROOT/history/price_5m/{BTC,ETH,SOL,XRP}.csv.gz`. Candidate assets shown in report, missing or insufficient-history assets listed as excluded. The loader's expected interval and invalid-return rules apply. This new independent research module does not import fitted Volatility Dynamics/AM-T-HAR-Blend model parameters or overwrite their results.
- Candle sign = sign of **close-to-close log return**. `r>0` means up, `r<0` down. **Not open-to-close candle body.** Exact zero is a third category: breaks a run and is reported, but is excluded from binary next-nonzero-sign forecasts. Gaps and invalid returns reset the current run and **right-censor** its last observed length (never count a missing next candle as a reversal). End-of-file also right-censored. Episode completion by an opposite sign or a zero is counted, with termination cause saved.
- `length_t`: 1 for the first observed nonzero return after a change/zero/gap, increasing by one only while consecutive **contiguous** five-minute returns have the same sign. `signed_streak_t = sign_t * length_t`.
- At every `t` where sign and length are known, define `next_valid` only if `t+5min` exists and is finite; separate `next_zero`, `next_continue`, `next_reverse`, `next_censored`. Main conditional probability `P(next sign equals current | next is nonzero, sign_t, length_t)` explicitly **conditions on the next return being nonzero**. Also report unconditional immediate-bar continuation probabilities including zero returns as terminations. If zero frequency proves material, build a full three-class model in a new preregistered module.
- The independent symmetric fair-sign hypothesis implies `P(+,+,+)=0.5^3=12.5%` for three **specified** bars, not the probability of finding an up triple *somewhere* in a multi-bar window. Under independence `P(next up | k prior ups)=0.5`, irrespective of `k`.

## Statistical baselines and sequence-dependent predictive study

- Describe exact completed-run count, censored run count and reached-k exposure count **at every observed positive/negative length**, without truncating rare long runs. Compare completed-run shape to approximate geometric law with empirical sign marginals, and simulate 100 within-asset iid-sign permutations retaining zero/missing timestamps, to report null ranges for maximum run and runs of length 8+ (the **8 threshold is a long-tail diagnostic**, not a data window, run cap or model feature ceiling).
- Chronological full-UTC-day split over all observed dates: 60% TRAIN, 20% VALID (diagnostics only), 20% TEST (frozen evaluation). A label that crosses any train/validation/test boundary is excluded from model fitting/scoring. Historical periods are already partially inspected in previous branches; this is exploratory, not fresh prospective evidence. Fitted probabilities never use future results.
- All main binary scores are conditional on a **nonzero immediately following five-minute bar**. Benchmarks, fitted TRAIN only: (a) fixed 50/50, (b) empirical unconditional up probability, (c) first-order Markov continuation by current sign. Proposed length-sensitive methods: (d) exact signed length (`+k`, `-k`) continuation frequency with 40 pseudo-observation shrinkage to Markov rate; unobserved lengths fall back to Markov; (e) logistic continuation model with sign, log(length), and their interaction, regularized ridge `.015` on length slopes. Hyperparameters fixed before examining historical outputs. No per-asset custom thresholds, K-selection, grid tuning or look-ahead.
- Primary model-value gate: on **BTC and ETH** TEST, require one length-sensitive model to improve next-nonzero-sign logloss over **both** Markov and unconditional-sign benchmarks, with the *paired complete-day bootstrap* 95% upper bound below zero for each asset. Brier, calibration and VALID results are reported but do not justify tuning based on TEST. Holdout gains would motivate a **new** explicit AM-T-HAR-Blend integration study; no model/pricing baseline is modified here. SOL/XRP are secondary replications if available.
- 1000 random-resampled UTC-day paired-bootstrap repetitions per comparison. Per-length Wilson intervals are descriptive only and may understate uncertainty under within-day dependence. No claim of independence between adjacent live-streak risk sets. Permutation nulls preserve sign counts but not intraday drift/serial dependence; do not call them formal generative p-values.

## Reporting and safety

Notebook `notebooks/Candle_Streaks/01_running_sign_streaks.ipynb` saves small, reproducible **aggregates only** to `notebooks/Candle_Streaks/01/`: manifest, per-asset quality, complete/censored distribution with **all observed lengths**, reached-length continuation risk table, chronological sign-model evaluation scores, paired bootstrap, null permutations, fitted-model compact parameters and a falsifiable decision. No raw price data, per-origin labels, large model checkpoints, collector/database edits or trade claims are uploaded.

For every interpretation: descriptive long streaks and a 50% theoretical benchmark do not by themselves imply a trading edge. The key evidence is whether sign length adds chronologically robust improvement beyond a simple first-order Markov model, and whether conditional sign improvement is useful for future density or execution decisions after transaction costs. AM-T-HAR-Blend is primarily a volatility/density model at potentially different forecast horizons; compare compatible horizon forecasts if future incorporation is requested.
