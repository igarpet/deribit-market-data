## Precise hypothesis

The return **itself**, rather than a Student-t innovation assigned to a volatility factor, is generated by a five-minute conditional Student-t Markov kernel. The state may be return-only (`r_t`) or augmented by the already-defined fast and slow filtered log-volatility variables (`r_t,L_t,F_t`). Does this physical-world *five-minute transition law*, propagated exactly in five-minute steps (subject to stated numerical integration error), improve predictive returns at 5, 10, 15, 30, 60, 120 and 240 minutes compared with VD14's original augmented Gaussian-mixture approximation and VD14's direct horizon-specific Student-t model?

**Critical conceptual distinction.** `r_{t+1} | (r_t,L_{t+1},F_{t+1})` is conditional Student-t. `r_{t+1} | r_t,L_t,F_t` is an integral over random next-period state. The k-bar cumulative return is a convolution of k native five-minute kernels. **Do not** simply re-fit a terminal-horizon Student-t distribution and call that a Markov return process. Historical return stochastic dependence is tested through `phi`, not assumed.

The return kernel is:

\[
 r_{t+1} = \phi_a r_t +\sqrt{c_a\exp(L_{t+1}+F_{t+1})}\,\sqrt{(\nu_a-2)/\nu_a}\,T_{\nu_a,t+1}, \quad \nu_a>2,
\]

with independent conditional t innovations. `(L,F)` follows a fitted two-dimensional Gaussian AR(1) with diagonal persistence and correlated state innovations (same parametric specification as VD14). Volatility state innovations are normal; **only returns have Student-t innovations**. No explicit jump component is added to AM-T. The Gaussian scale-mixture representation of Student-t is used only as a numerical device to integrate future price returns, not as a different underlying model. Location term has no intercept, consistent with the empirical zero-drift null.

## Evaluation and Monte Carlo integration

For Student-t innovations, the identity \(\sqrt{(\nu-2)/\chi_\nu^2}\,Z\) allows integrating future t innovations as mixtures of conditional Gaussians. The return-AR(1) cumulative-innovation weight from step j to k is \((1-\phi^{k-j+1})/(1-\phi)\). Integrating over Gaussian state-transition paths and inverse-chi-square latent scales yields a numerical predictive density for the k-step return. Each particle defines a *Gaussian conditional distribution*, so density/tail evaluation is Rao-Blackwellized; returns themselves are not sampled in a noisy histogram/KDE.

- **Numerical integration:** 256 scrambled-Sobol particles per horizon in blocks of 2048 origins; deterministic seeds and shared integration nodes for model comparisons. No cross-asset market-data leakage; shared particles are quadrature nodes, not real shared market shocks. QMC approximation error is monitored separately using 256 vs 512 particles on a fixed up-to-512-origins-per-asset **VALIDATION** slice at 60m. Large QMC changes would invalidate small NLL differences; do not tune model specification to test-period results.
- **Primary metric:** mean held-out negative log-likelihood of signed net returns, smaller better. Same-origin bootstrap over **whole UTC days** across assets, 1000 draws; this respects within-day origin overlap and cross-asset alignment but may not capture cross-day dependence fully.
- **Secondary:** tail-event log loss and Brier for TRAIN q95 |R_h|; model-implied tail forecast versus observed event rate; nominal 95% central interval miss-rate; per-asset and per-horizon decomposition; numerical particle stability.
- **Pre-registered NLL comparisons (at 60m):** T_MC−T_RW, AM_T−AM_G, AM_T−M2, AM_T_AR−AM_T, AM_T_AR−M2, AM_G−M2. A candidate `AM_T` improvement requires upper 95% day-bootstrap CI below 0 against **both** AM_G and M2 and examination of tail losses/calibration. AM_T_AR is separate secondary evidence, not an automatic replacement. There are six comparisons; unadjusted confidence intervals should not be read as family-wise error controlled. Six other horizons are descriptive.
- No result from the already-viewed 2024–2026 history may be marked PROSPECTIVELY CONFIRMED; genuine confirmation requires new post-2026-10-14 observations, a new cutoff and frozen specification.

## Data quality and interpretive limits

The state `(L,F)` consists of **filtered price-derived observations**, not identified latent structural regimes; exact Markovity is imposed on the approximating model, not empirically proven. The model assumes independence between return Student-t scale shocks and volatility-transition shocks; leverage/feedback may violate this. Student-t has finite variance only for `nu>2` and for `nu<=4` has infinite fourth moment, making rare tails and loss variance estimation delicate. Five-minute spot bar data cannot identify genuine continuous-time jumps, so no discontinuity or options-tradability claims. The experiment cannot by itself validate HMM/HSMM regimes, risk-neutral pricing or 0DTE profitability.

Training on the same previously inspected history is exploratory; earlier AVAX historical data-quality warnings apply. All predictions are made ex-ante with no future volatility observations. The MC integration remains a finite-particle numerical approximation; tail stability must be checked. No system collectors, DuckDB, or other branches are modified.
