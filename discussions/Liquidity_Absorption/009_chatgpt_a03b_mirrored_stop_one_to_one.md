## 1. Hypothesis and exact one-field intervention

The original A03 was an exploratory three-policy execution study of 452 price-only second-touch events, retaining their A02/A02b order-flow features. **A03b changes only the INITIAL STOP PRICE**. The current A03 target-selection rule (signal-close UTC VWAP if at least two *original* A03 risk units away, otherwise opposing prior-day extreme) is preserved. The original A03 first post-close recorded print and its entry cutoff are preserved. Thus no circular target selection occurs when the risk is changed.

Let the first recorded entry print be \(E\), the **already selected A03** take-profit be \(T\), and direction be \(d=+1\) long or \(d=-1\) short. When \(d(T-E)>0\), A03b sets:

\[
S_{1:1}=E-(T-E)=2E-T,\quad R=|T-E|,\quad \frac{|T-E|}{|E-S_{1:1}|}=1.
\]

Example: long E=60,000; original structural TP=60,600; mirrored SL=59,400. **TP remains 60,600.** This is exact 1:1 geometric RR at the observed first print, **before** fees/impact, not 1:1 net trading economics. A stop gap can lose more than -1R; an exit by 240m timeout need not be +/-1R.

**Important fidelity limitation:** the mirrored stop is NOT forced to the original defended wick or zone. It may be far from that zone, or inside another level. Mirroring uses a continuous unrounded price so equality is exact; actual tick-validity is not proven. This A03b is a **stop-to-target sensitivity** rather than a faithful implementation of the original MNQ stop instructions.

## 2. Interaction with BE/structural trailing

A03's unchanged BE activation is at +1.0 *initial R*. When TP is precisely +1R, the TP is reached at the same print that first qualifies BE, so BE cannot protect the position **before** TP; subsequent HH/HL trailing is likewise inactive. Therefore A03b reports one substantive new execution policy: **FIXED mirrored stop**. For regression safety it evaluates the inherited three policies internally and **asserts outcome equivalence** rather than presenting redundant policy curves. Moving BE to +0.5R would be a **different strategy**, requiring an explicit separate comparison.

## 4. Equity curve operational assumptions

**Graphs are not true account equity curves:** A02 candidates may overlap, we have no position sizing, capital reservation, portfolio constraints, mark-to-market of open trades or funding, and historical prints do not prove executable fills. The closest reproducible proxy is **realized cumulative P&L of a hypothetical USDT 10,000 fixed notional allocated to each separate candidate**. Each trade closes and contributes its gain/loss at its own **actual simulated exit time**. Baseline and A03b lines only contain candidate IDs with a complete exit in BOTH versions, avoiding unfair selective censoring. A separate 1:1 gross/net **cumulative R** graph retains the variable **A03b risk** denominator, which is different from the original A03 risk; direct cross-policy R sums must not be interpreted as equal-risk portfolio comparisons.

**Source-file boundary:** Prior A03 reported no Oct 1 trade files for BTC/ETH. The wider 1:1 stop may keep a trade open for longer; any position lacking observed prints through its 240m deadline is **CENSORED_TAPE_END**, not assumed closed. The event appears in the full event file but is not part of a paired completed-outcome graph. Later local October trade files may improve coverage if legitimately available.
